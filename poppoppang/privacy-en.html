1. Summary

Pop Pop Pang has no accounts, and we (Younggun Games) run no servers. Your game records and settings are stored only on your device.

However, Google processes the information below for ads (Google AdMob) and rankings (Google Play Games Services), and Unity processes it to fix errors and crashes.

3. Information processed by Google and Unity

Ads — Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

  • What may be collected: device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android advertising ID), approximate location inferred from the IP address, ad interactions (ads seen and tapped), device and app information, and error and performance information.
  • Purpose: showing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud, and analytics.
  • There are only two kinds of ads: a rewarded ad (+15 seconds to keep playing, once per game) that you choose with a button when time runs out, and an interstitial ad shown when you tap "Retry" on the result screen (not after your first game since install). No ads appear while you are playing, and there are no banner ads.
  • Residents of certain US states: in places covered by state privacy laws such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and Utah, providing information such as the advertising ID to Google for personalized ads may count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under those laws. We do not sell personal information for money. If you delete your advertising ID in your device settings, it is not used for personalized ads. Send requests to access, delete or correct information to the contact email below. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.
  • More: Google Privacy Policy https://policies.google.com/privacy , How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • To turn off personalized ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Rankings — Google Play Games Services

  • We use Google Play Games Services for the ranking screen and leaderboard. When the game starts it signs in to Play Games automatically (if this fails, you can still play), and when a game ends it sends that game's score. The level of that game is sent with the score as a short tag (for example "L12").
  • Google processes: your Play Games account name (nickname), game ID (player ID), and the score (with the level of that game), under Google's Privacy Policy https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Other players see only your nickname, score and level. We don't use profile pictures.
  • To leave the rankings, change your game activity visibility in the Play Games app settings, or email us below.

Errors and crashes — Unity Diagnostics (Unity Technologies, as our processor)

  • Sent: technical information when the app crashes, stops responding or logs an error — the error and where it happened (stack trace), the app version, device model, OS version and other technical device and memory information that the Unity engine attaches.
  • Not sent: anything that identifies you, such as your name, email or advertising ID, and your game save file.
  • Purpose: fixing errors and improving stability. Never used for ads or marketing. Unity processes this information on our behalf as a processor; in Unity's developer data settings we receive required data only, with model contribution and business recommendation use turned off.
  • Turning it off: there is no separate switch in the app. It contains nothing that identifies you and is sent only when an error happens.
  • Retention: a report from a crash stays on the device and is sent the next time you open the app. Unity keeps reports according to Unity's retention policy, and we will delete them from the dashboard on request.
  • More: Unity Privacy Policy https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

4. In-app purchases

Pop Pop Pang currently sells nothing inside the app (no Google Play in-app purchases). So we neither receive nor process payment information, and the app does not connect to Google Play Billing. If we add in-app purchases later, we will update this policy first and post it with a new effective date.

5. Children

Pop Pop Pang is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not designed for children, and ad requests are not tagged as child-directed. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data: deleted immediately when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data in your device settings.
  • Data processed by Google: subject to Google's retention policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Leaderboard records: delete this game's data in the Play Games app, or email us and we will remove the record in the Play Console.
  • Error reports processed by Unity: kept according to Unity's retention policy; if you ask, we will delete them from the dashboard. Email us below.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
