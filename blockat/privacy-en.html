1. Summary

Blockat has no accounts and we (Younggun Games) run no servers. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. For ads (Google AdMob) and the in-app purchase (Google Play Billing), Google processes the information described below.

2. What we store on your device

3. Information processed by Google

Ads — Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and the User Messaging Platform (UMP)

  • May be collected: device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions (ads viewed or tapped), device and app information, crash and performance data.
  • Purposes: serving ads, measuring ads, preventing fraud, analytics.
  • Consent: in regions that require it (EEA, UK, Switzerland, …) Google's consent form appears on first launch. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Remove Ads → "Privacy & Ads".
  • Ads are only rewarded ads you choose by tapping a button, and occasional full-screen ads between days or when leaving the result screen. There are no banner ads.
  • More: Google Privacy Policy https://policies.google.com/privacy , How Google uses information from partner sites https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • To limit personalized ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Google → Ads.

Payments — Google Play Billing

  • The "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • The app only asks Google Play whether this item was bought (including Restore).

4. If you bought "Remove Ads"

The app stops loading ads. Rewards are granted right away without ads.

5. Children

Blockat is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and ad requests are not tagged as child-directed. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data: deleted immediately with "Erase All Records" or by uninstalling the app.
  • Data processed by Google: subject to Google's retention policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

7. Changes

If this policy changes we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
