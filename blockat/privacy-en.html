1. Summary

Blockat has no accounts and we (Younggun Games) run no servers. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.

For ads (Google AdMob) and the in-app purchase (Google Play Billing), Google processes the information described below, and Unity processes error and crash information so we can fix problems.

3. Information processed by Google and Unity

Ads — Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and the User Messaging Platform (UMP)

  • May be collected: device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions (ads viewed or tapped), device and app information, crash and performance data.
  • Purposes: serving ads, measuring ads, preventing fraud, analytics.
  • Consent: in regions that require it (EEA, UK, Switzerland, …) Google's consent form appears on first launch. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Remove Ads → "Privacy & Ads".
  • Ads are only rewarded ads you choose by tapping a button, and one full-screen ad when a new weekday starts. There are no full-screen ads during play, in the workshop or on the result screen, and no banner ads.
  • More: Google Privacy Policy https://policies.google.com/privacy , How Google uses information from partner sites https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • To limit personalized ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Google → Ads.

Errors and crashes — Unity Diagnostics (Unity Technologies, as our processor)

  • Sent: technical information when the app crashes, stops responding or logs an error — the error and where it happened (stack trace), plus these 8 items: app version, version code, build type (development / release), device model, OS version, game language, the name of the screen where it happened and a short seed of that game. The Unity engine may attach technical device details such as memory.
  • Not sent: your name, email, advertising ID or anything else that identifies you, and your save files.
  • Purpose: fixing errors and improving stability. Never used for ads or marketing. Unity processes this information on our behalf; in Unity's developer data settings we collect required data only and have turned off use for model training and business recommendations.
  • Opting out: there is no in-app switch; nothing identifies you, and reports are sent only when an error happens.
  • Retention: a crash report is kept on the device and sent the next time you open the app. Unity keeps reports according to Unity's retention policy, and we will delete them from the dashboard on request.
  • More: Unity Privacy Policy https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Payments — Google Play Billing

  • The "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • The app only asks Google Play whether this item was bought (including Restore).

4. If you bought "Remove Ads"

The app stops loading ads. Rewards are granted right away without ads.

5. Children

Blockat is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and ad requests are not tagged as child-directed. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data: deleted immediately with "Erase All Records" or by uninstalling the app.
  • Data processed by Google: subject to Google's retention policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Error reports processed by Unity: kept according to Unity's retention policy; if you ask, we will delete them from the dashboard. Email us below.

7. Changes

If this policy changes we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
